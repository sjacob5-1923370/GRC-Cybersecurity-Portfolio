# Enterprise Project Risk Management

## Overview

Project risk management plan developed for a simulated business engagement involving LSDS and ASDG. The project focused on identifying, evaluating, prioritizing, documenting, and monitoring risks that could affect project delivery.

The plan covers financial, technical, operational, organizational, reputational, strategic, and compliance risks and includes both qualitative and quantitative risk analysis.

## Project Scope

The risk management plan includes:

- Risk identification and categorization
- Qualitative risk analysis
- Quantitative risk analysis
- Probability-impact assessment
- Risk response planning
- Stakeholder risk tolerance analysis
- Cost and schedule contingency planning
- Risk ownership and status tracking
- Risk reporting and monitoring
- Quality assurance and quality control considerations

## Key Deliverables

### Risk Register
Created a structured risk register documenting:

- Risk description
- Risk category
- Impacted project constraints
- Threat or opportunity classification
- Probability
- Impact
- Expected Monetary Value (EMV)
- Response strategy
- Risk owner
- Status

### Quantitative Risk Analysis
Used Expected Monetary Value (EMV) to estimate financial exposure for identified risks.

The analysis resulted in a calculated contingency reserve of **$20,900** for the risks evaluated.

### Schedule Risk Analysis
Applied probability and schedule impact estimates to calculate contingency time for identified schedule risks.

The analysis produced **16.4 days of schedule contingency**.

### Risk Prioritization
Used probability and impact ratings to prioritize risks based on likelihood and potential effect on project objectives.

### Risk Response Planning
Developed response strategies including:

- Mitigation
- Acceptance
- Transfer
- Avoidance
- Enhancement
- Exploitation

### Stakeholder Risk Analysis
Evaluated stakeholder roles, risk attitudes, expectations, requirements, and potential influence on project outcomes.

### Risk Monitoring and Reporting
Defined processes for:

- Risk register updates
- Status tracking
- Mitigation progress
- Ownership changes
- Emerging risk identification
- Stakeholder reporting
- Lessons learned
