# Crisis Management Plan

## Overview

Crisis management and business continuity plan developed for a simulated organization to support preparation, response, communication, and recovery during operational disruptions.

The plan addresses natural disasters, technology incidents, workplace violence, health emergencies, and data breaches, with defined escalation procedures, stakeholder responsibilities, communication methods, and recovery actions.

## Project Scope

The crisis management plan includes:

- Crisis identification and activation criteria
- Severity and urgency levels
- Escalation procedures
- Crisis response roles and responsibilities
- Internal and external communication planning
- Business continuity considerations
- Incident response scenarios
- Command center planning
- Resource and emergency supply planning
- Employee training and exercises
- Post-incident review and lessons learned

## Crisis Activation and Escalation

Defined activation triggers for events including:

- Severe weather and natural disasters
- Security breaches
- Workplace violence
- Public health emergencies
- Threats to life and safety
- Equipment, system, and power failures

Established three levels of urgency:

- Level 1 — Minor
- Level 2 — Moderate
- Level 3 — Major

The plan also defines an escalation process from initial assessment and notification through response activation, continuous monitoring, resolution, and recovery.

## Crisis Response Scenarios

Developed response guidance for multiple crisis types:

### Natural Disaster
Addressed preparedness, employee safety, backup operations, emergency communications, damage assessment, and recovery.

### Workplace Violence
Defined immediate safety actions, law enforcement coordination, employee communication, post-incident support, and review of safety procedures.

### Pandemic / Health Emergency
Covered health guidance, policy compliance, employee communication, and coordination with legal and public health requirements.

### Data Breach
Outlined identification, containment, notification, investigation, recovery, vulnerability remediation, and post-incident review.

## Crisis Management Structure

Defined a crisis management chain of command with roles including:

- Crisis Management Team Leader
- Operations Coordinator
- Communications Coordinator
- Safety Officer
- Logistics Coordinator
- Site-Specific Response Teams

The plan also includes primary and backup command center requirements, equipment needs, emergency supplies, and access to critical documents.

## Communication Planning

Developed internal and external communication procedures covering:

- Company-wide emergency notifications
- Backup communication channels
- Stakeholder updates
- Secure communication of sensitive information
- Public relations coordination
- Regulatory and customer notification
- Holding statements
- Crisis hotlines and information resources

A communication flow was established between leadership, Human Resources, IT, Legal / Public Relations, employees, stakeholders, and media.

## Response Timeline

Defined expected actions across the first stages of a crisis:

- **0–15 minutes:** Identify the crisis, assemble the response team, and begin response actions
- **15–60 minutes:** Assess impact, communicate with stakeholders, and activate emergency procedures
- **1–3 hours:** Continue response actions, monitor conditions, and provide status updates
- **3–4 hours:** Begin recovery activities while continuing monitoring
