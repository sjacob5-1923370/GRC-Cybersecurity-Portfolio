# Squid Game Cryptocurrency Token Report

## Overview
Cybersecurity and fraud analysis of the 2021 Squid Game cryptocurrency scam, a rug-pull scheme that used the popularity of the Netflix series to attract investors.
The project examined how the fraud operated, the security and financial risks involved, the role of social media and misleading promotion, investigative methods used in cryptocurrency fraud cases, and measures that could help reduce similar scams.

## Areas Analyzed
- Cryptocurrency fraud and rug-pull schemes
- Smart contract restrictions
- Social engineering and deceptive marketing
- Financial and reputational risk
- Media and social media influence
- Cryptocurrency investigation methods
- Regulatory and ethical considerations
- Fraud prevention and investor protection

## Key Findings

### Fraud Mechanics
The SQUID token was promoted as part of a play-to-earn game tied to the popularity of the Squid Game television series.

The project identified several major warning signs:
- Anonymous developers
- Misleading claims regarding the project
- Heavy reliance on social media and hype
- Restrictions preventing many investors from selling their tokens
- Smart contract controls that allowed selected wallets to cash out

The developers ultimately disappeared with approximately **$3.38 million** in investor funds.

### Smart Contract Risk
The report examined how the token's smart contract included restrictions that prevented many buyers from selling their holdings while allowing wallets controlled by the developers to withdraw funds.
This demonstrated how technical controls within a cryptocurrency project can create significant financial and fraud risk when investors do not understand how the underlying contract operates.

### Investigative Methods Reviewed
The project examined investigative techniques used by authorities and cybersecurity professionals in cryptocurrency fraud investigations, including:

- Digital forensics
- Blockchain and transaction tracing
- Network / link analysis
- Smart contract auditing
- Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT)
- Analysis of social media, online forums, and messaging platforms

## Risk and Business Impact

The analysis considered several consequences of the fraud:

- Investor financial losses
- Reputational damage to cryptocurrency projects
- Loss of investor trust
- Legal and regulatory concerns
- Increased scrutiny of anonymous cryptocurrency projects
- Risks associated with poorly vetted digital assets

## Ethical and Regulatory Considerations

The project also examined the tension between investor protection and decentralized financial innovation.

Topics included:

- Misleading marketing
- Anonymous project developers
- Investor vulnerability
- Cryptocurrency regulation
- Transparency requirements
- Privacy and decentralization
- The role of artificial intelligence in fraud detection

## Recommendations

The report proposed several approaches for reducing cryptocurrency fraud risk:

1. Stronger Know Your Customer (KYC) controls on cryptocurrency exchanges
2. Blockchain analytics and automated fraud detection
3. Public education on cryptocurrency scam indicators
4. Clearer regulatory enforcement
5. Increased collaboration between cybersecurity firms, blockchain specialists, financial institutions, and law enforcement
